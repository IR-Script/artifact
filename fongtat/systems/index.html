Fong Tat: High-Level Systems Design

The systems Fong Tat will run across its three units, how they connect, how the main processes run through them, and what we still need to confirm with the client. All components are off the shelf. Integrated Retail delivers the Oppein POS and its integrations, and supervises the rest.

Scope at a glance

BU 1 · Workers' dormitoryOne vendor package, Aupro or iSense: dormitory management, face turnstiles, CCTV, Wi-Fi and PA. Installed fully by the vendor. IR supervises the feed into Xero.
Core officeXero for accounting across all entities, and an off-the-shelf HRMS, Great HR or Zoho HR. The building maintenance system is requested but not yet scoped.
BU 3 · Oppein Home + MK ServicesIR core delivery: POS on Retail Pro Prism or Cegid, with CRM and inventory inside the POS. The Oppein design system supplies the BOM. MK installation jobs run in Xero Projects.

Design rules

Off the shelf onlyNo core transaction system is built. IR configures, integrates and supervises.
POS is the stock masterStock quantities live in the POS. Xero receives stock value and cost of sales only, so stock is never kept in two places.
One billing rule for Oppein customersDeposits, sales invoices and final payments come from the POS. Change orders are invoiced from Xero against the same project. To confirm with the client.
One project IDCreated once in the POS when the order is confirmed and reused in Xero Projects and on every invoice.
Biometric and KYC data stay with the vendorFace templates and worker KYC stay in the dormitory system. Only occupancy and invoice data leave it, in line with PDPC guidance.
Separate logins and dashboardsThere is no single sign-on. Each system keeps its own users, roles and dashboard. A consolidated dashboard is optional.
